What if I don't like the 24-hour free demo?
There is zero obligation. If you don't like the design or layout of the free demo we build for your clinic, you simply don't pay anything. It costs you nothing to see exactly how your clinic could look online.
What if I just want a WhatsApp button and not a complex system?
The Clinic Starter (WhatsApp Connect) plan is perfect for this. We build your premium mobile site, and when a patient books, it opens a pre-filled WhatsApp message directly to your clinic number. ₹10k setup + ₹5k/year.
How do I upgrade from WhatsApp to a full dashboard later?
Seamlessly. You can move up to the Smart Queue or Admin Dashboard tiers anytime. The AMC simply adjusts to the new tier, and we migrate your existing data to the new system.
Will this website automatically bring me new patients?
A website is the engine, but Google Maps is the fuel. While a fast, mobile-friendly website significantly boosts your credibility, you also need to be visible when patients search "Doctor near me."

That is why we offer our Local Visibility Package as an add-on. We optimize your Google Business Profile, manage your clinical services, and link it to your new custom website to help you rank above corporate hospitals in local searches.
