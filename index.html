Common Deployment Questions
What if I just want a simple booking button and not a complex dashboard?
Our WhatsApp Bridge architecture is designed exactly for this. We deploy a premium mobile interface, and when a patient books, it routes a securely formatted message directly to your clinic's existing WhatsApp number. Deployment: ₹10k. Maintenance: ₹3k/year.
Is it possible to scale to a full database system later?
Absolutely. Our platforms are modular. You can seamlessly migrate to the Smart Queue or Admin Dashboard infrastructure at any time without data loss or downtime.
What is the typical deployment timeline?
Upon receiving your clinic's assets (branding, doctor profiles, facility imagery), our engineering team provisions a live sandbox environment for your review within 24 hours.
